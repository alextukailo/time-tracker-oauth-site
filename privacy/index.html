About this application

Time Tracker is a Chrome extension for tracking work sessions by project and synchronizing records with Google Sheets. This policy describes the version of the extension using Google OAuth and the Google Sheets API.

Developer contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER.

Google data we access

With your consent, Time Tracker receives an OAuth access token for the Google Sheets API with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets. This scope technically permits reading and modifying spreadsheets that your Google account can access, even though Time Tracker's synchronization feature works with the spreadsheet you explicitly connect.

The extension processes the connected spreadsheet's title and identifier, project names, session dates and start/end times, durations, task descriptions, and technical identifiers needed to synchronize and delete records.

We never ask for your Google password.

How we use data

We use this information only to provide user-facing time tracking features: recording work sessions, showing history, synchronizing a selected spreadsheet, and updating or deleting records when you request it.

The Google Sheets API is used to read from and write to your connected spreadsheet. Data is not used for advertising, sold, or used for credit assessment.

Where data is stored

Projects, sessions, connected spreadsheet settings, and pending changes are stored in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Synchronized records are stored in the Google spreadsheet you select, under your Google account.

Authentication uses the Chrome Identity API. Time Tracker does not store your Google password. In the current architecture, the extension calls the Google Sheets API directly and does not operate a separate backend for storing your project or spreadsheet content.

Sharing and transfers

Time Tracker sends the necessary information to Google via the Google Sheets API over HTTPS to carry out the synchronization you request. The developer does not sell user data, share it with advertising networks, or disclose it to data brokers for marketing.

The public website may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider that processes standard technical requests under its own privacy policy. This website does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Google API Limited Use

Our use of information received through Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to support the prominent, user-facing functionality of Time Tracker.

Use of information received from Google APIs also adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete time sessions and projects from the Time Tracker interface, and change or remove data directly in Google Sheets. Disconnecting your account or uninstalling the extension does not automatically remove content already saved in your Google Sheet.

You can revoke access at Google Account — Connections. To remove local data, clear the extension's stored data or uninstall it from Chrome. To remove data stored in Google Sheets, delete the relevant rows or spreadsheet in your Google account.

Security

Requests to the Google Sheets API use HTTPS. Access to your spreadsheets depends on your Google account's permissions and the OAuth access you grant. Only connect spreadsheets that you want to use for time tracking.

Policy updates

If our data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated. Material changes requiring additional consent will be explained within the product before they take effect.
